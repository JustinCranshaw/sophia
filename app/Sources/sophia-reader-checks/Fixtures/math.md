## The graph Laplacian

For **undirected graphs**, $L = D - A$ and $x^\top Lx \geq 0$.
Inline fractions: $\frac{a}{b}$; bracket notation: \(\lambda_2\).

$$
\frac{x^\top Lx}{x^\top x} = \frac{\sum_{(i,j)\in E}(x_i-x_j)^2}{\sum_i x_i^2}
$$

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

\[
\begin{aligned}
Lx &= \lambda x \\
x^\top Lx &= \lambda x^\top x
\end{aligned}
\]

- Eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda_i \geq 0$.
- Old Unicode still reads: xᵀLx, λ₂, ∑.
