What it says
Carved-letter proportions in a sans. The flared strokes swell and taper the way the petals round off against their constant channels. It is clearly neither New York nor SF.
Live risk
All three named the same risk: it can read as cosmetics, spa or memorial lettering rather than a notebook. At 16px its thin joins may look spindly beside the solid plum.
Availability

How this was made

  • Fonts. Every face was checked against the installed-font catalog of the Mac that built this page, using AppKit (NSFontManager) on 26 October 2026. On that Mac, WebKit's drawing of each family is pixel-identical to the named PostScript face and differs from the generic fallback. Not installed there: Iowan Old Style, Athelas, Seravek, Superclarendon. The page uses only local fonts and loads nothing over the network. If a face is missing in the browser that opens this page, the card says so, because the specimen is then showing a fallback.
  • Licensing. On the build Mac all four faces are in the system font folder. Nobody has checked whether their licences allow outlining the word into an SVG or PNG logo for README or web use. That question is open.
  • Sizing. Each face's size and vertical shift come from its CoreText ascent, descent and cap height, so the cap heights match across options. Option 3 centres its lowercase between the x-height and the cap height.
  • Process. This was a three-voice charrette (Mara for design, Marcus for product, Imani for CEO) in divergent mode, with a model of claude-opus-5-5 configured at High effort. The voices reported claude-opus-5-5 from their own context and could not observe the effective effort. No voice and no lead picks a winner; the choice is the user's.
  • Not done. Nothing is applied to the Mac app. The wordmark has not been tested with people.
