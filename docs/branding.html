Sophia — four branding directions

A first prototype for comparison. It is not a decision and there is no winner. Every sample text is illustrative: there are no real sources, quotations or results. Fonts come from the system faces installed on this Mac (Charter, Optima, Palatino, Big Caslon, Hoefler Text, Avenir Next, Menlo, plus system-ui and ui-serif) and nothing loads over a network. Each direction owns a different primary object (the page, the voice, the reading, the claim), so each card's samples take a different shape. Cards always render on their light palette; dark versions are not drawn yet.

Considered, not drawn: Open Question

In critique, two of the three voices called this a real gap: no card makes the person’s question the main unit, even though inquiry need not produce a page. The idea would head each notebook and Almanac entry with the question that was taken up, never promise answers, and never use a question mark as a prompt to reply. It was not folded into one of the four, which would have blurred both. It is the next direction to draw if the set needs one.

This is a prototype exploration for comparison. It changes no product branding and makes no trademark or availability claims. Page and conversation behaviour belongs to SOP59. Sophia writes pages directly and you can Undo; there is no draft or acceptance step, and no direction adds a routine “saved” banner. In every direction, status colour is kept for rare cases: a goal Sophia noted that stays provisional until you confirm it (pages are never held for approval), or an error.
