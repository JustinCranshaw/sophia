Gloss

The brand is the architecture of commentary around a text. Your page is the central column; Sophia speaks in the margin, tied to the line she comments on by a small rubric reference point.

Fixed context, copied unchanged from the previous supplied-SVG comparison. Only the Sophia mark changes: the selected form, orientation and mark color. The Almanac masthead mark stays ink-only. The facts below are verbatim round-two text and describe round-two marks that are not shown here.

Why the marks belong
Gloss bracket (Sophia): a heavy column (the text), a square reference point on its top line, and a thin bracket for commentary. Surround (Almanac): the text block closed on three sides by commentary — a finished, framed entry, open only at the foot. Both draw a reading relation, not an object.
Provenance roles
Source = indented block with SOURCE numeral; Yours = heavy left rule + YOURS; Sophia = smaller roman in the gloss column with rubric small-caps label. Rubric appears on labels, reference points and the selection; error uses ochre + ⚠ + words. Dark is not an inversion: hairlines vanish at night, so the margin becomes a raised plane on cool graphite. Below 620px the gloss stays beside its line as an indented gloss with a hanging rubric letter.
Type
Hoefler Text (fallback Baskerville, Georgia) roman/italic/small caps; UI labels in the same family.
Excluded
No manuscript texture, blackletter, daggers/pilcrows as ornament, or religious decoration.
Risk & test
At 16px the bracket may read as code. Rubric red may read as a correction pen. Test greyscale 16px, and whether the 390px interleaved gloss (hanging rubric letters) still reads as commentary.

Process

This was a standard three-voice charrette (Mara, design; Marcus, product; Imani, CEO), divergent within the chosen family. The lead, Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5), drew every derived form and wrote this page. A closure round inspected the revised renders. Each voice was read-only and reported claude-opus-5-5 as its model. The agent definitions configure High effort; the effective level was not reported. The runtime cannot resume agents, so the critique and closure rounds used disclosed replacement voices. Each replacement was given its own earlier position and digests of the other two.

  • Openings, from the supplied render: all three found the supplied gap narrows from about 8 to about 4 units toward the centre. They proposed DR Rosette, DT Domed Taper and DU Upright Scallop, and each argued for context-accent color only. The lead added DC as a control.
  • Rendered critique, from the first build’s renders: all three read the 16px strip as five near-identical rings. Mara and Marcus read DT’s inner corners as kinked. Imani and Marcus read DU’s flat cuts at 3 and 9 o’clock as a cog, and Mara read its inner corners as hooked. Marcus and Imani saw the Gloss accent on the mark matching Gloss’s annotation red. Each voice then reversed its color position and proposed a mark-owned hue.
  • Revision: DR, DT and DU were replaced by each voice’s own revised form (RS, PT, TP). Rotation became an orientation switch, as Marcus proposed. Delft, Plum and Madder were added. Context accents moved into a collapsed row.
  • Closure, from the revised renders: Mara found a nick at RS’s inner ends, which r4 inner corners fixed. Marcus judged the set bounded and the order of choice to be form, then orientation, then color. Imani confirmed the supplied baseline stays distinct and kept Madder with a Gloss caveat. All three accept that the forms remain alike at 16px.
  • Tonal round (scope correction: color on the mark, including one tonal relationship): the lead built a mirrored vertical ramp of each owned hue, held constant across the four contexts. From the renders, all three found the light ramps too deep at the bottom; Mara’s narrowed endpoints were applied. Mara and Marcus asked for solid below 24px. Marcus asked for Tonal to be disabled for non-owned colors. A reverse cell that wrongly picked up tonal paint was fixed before the critique. Shapes stayed frozen.

Static only, no motion. The geometry and this page were generated by small helper scripts in the lead’s scratch directory, which are not part of the repository. No ownership, novelty or trademark-clearance claim is made.
